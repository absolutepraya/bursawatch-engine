# Bursawatch: watch the sources, keep the judgment

## Founding belief

Following the market should not mean living in the feed. People need continuity and context, not another stream of urgent notifications.

## Vision

Make Indonesia’s markets easier to follow without giving up your attention or your judgment.

## Mission

Watch the sources people choose, identify relevant changes, preserve the evidence, and deliver a clear brief in the channels they already use.

## Promise

**Know what changed.**

The product handles attention, not conviction. It must not promise wealth, certain predictions or automated trading. A source opinion remains a source opinion. User preferences guide what is followed and how it is explained, never what counts as a fact.

## Why this follows the code

Hermes separates deterministic intake, state, filtering, deduplication and delivery from bounded model work. Some source-only workflows do not use an LLM at all. That makes dependable observation and source fidelity stronger foundations than an AI model name. The friend’s migration turns personal watching rules into a product others can configure; the website is the place users express those rules and understand the result.

1. **Attention is finite.** Filter for relevance; do not reward notification volume.
2. **Evidence comes first.** Preserve source facts, analyst attribution and missing-data states.
3. **Continuity builds trust.** Avoid repeated messages and show whether a source is healthy. Silence alone does not prove nothing happened.
4. **The user keeps agency.** Let people choose firms, horizons and explanation style, then pause or change them.
5. **Delivery completes the job.** A generated summary is not a delivered message; logs must distinguish them.

## Bird or abstract mark?

The existing bird was a provisional identity, not an established founding story. A watchful messenger fits the product, but an upward-flying silhouette can accidentally suggest speed or guaranteed returns. Hermes provides a useful messenger association, not a requirement to draw a literal bird or copy mythological wings.

The bouldering-club reference succeeds because the action is immediately legible. Carry over that economy and clarity, not the panda or its color scheme.

## Approved identity: Signal Fold

An abstract B made from two open folds. Separate left-hand openings suggest incoming sources; the folds gather into a shared outer edge, suggesting a coherent brief. The negative space is essential: selection means leaving irrelevant material out. The two layers may suggest wings, but neither an animal nor a letter needs to be recognized first.

This is a design intention, not a claim that every viewer will decode the philosophy without explanation. Test the silhouette, small-size clarity and recall first. Avoid forcing an eye, candlestick, B, W, bird and price arrow into one symbol.

The first exploration is archived as `public/brand/bursawatch-signal-fold-concept.svg`. The approved production mark is `public/brand/bursawatch-mark.svg`, using refinement 02. No trademark clearance has been performed.

### Refinement 02 — user feedback

The user selected “Refine Signal Fold,” said the direction was good, and asked for more product-related detail. They ended the Lavish review; do not reopen it without a new request. The refinement adds distinct source inlets, a central diagonal fold, and a third lower layer, keeping a single-color abstract B/wing silhouette. The explanation is a metaphor for the product workflow, not a literal data-flow diagram or a claim of immediate viewer recognition.

The user subsequently approved refinement 02 for implementation on 17 September 2026. `public/brand/bursawatch-signal-fold-v2.svg` preserves the reviewed artwork; `bursawatch-signal-fold-v2-board.svg` shows its rationale and applications. The production `BrandMark`, downloadable mark, favicon and avatars now use the same three paths. A two-unit translation centers the artwork in its 96-unit canvas without changing its construction.

## Application rules

- App navigation: 32px canvas, approximately 27px painted height, single color. Wordmark remains Hanken Grotesk. The home link retains a 44px minimum target and one accessible label.
- Avatar: center at 55–60% of a square or circular charcoal field; test a 32px crop.
- Favicon: the same three paths on charcoal, centered in a 96-unit canvas. Check at 16px and 32px; preserve all three source inlets.
- Exports: `bursawatch-avatar.svg` is the avatar master. The unused historical PNG chat export is not included in this migration; the Apple touch icon is retained. Keep future exports centered within circular crops.
- Briefs: identify Bursawatch as the delivery product; show the original research provider separately. Never replace a source logo with the Bursawatch mark.
- Colors: copper identity, charcoal surface, near-white type; semantic green remains for data/status, never the brand’s growth promise.
- Motion: no perpetual scanning or flapping. Confirm an actual state change once.
- Voice: short, concrete, calm. “Configuration saved.” “Source unavailable.” “Price changed 3.6%.” Avoid “unlock alpha,” “get rich,” and “AI-powered financial revolution.”
