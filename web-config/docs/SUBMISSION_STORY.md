# Show the workflow, then the polish

## Creative direction

The strongest hook is the user's present-day problem: too many sources, too
little context. Avoid a long stock-exchange history montage, accusations against
named influencers, or promises to prevent losses. Those claims distract from
what Bursawatch can prove. Frame it as information and analysis, not a stock tip
service or a substitute for investment advice.

Suggested Indonesian opening:

> “Satu saham. Banyak sumber. Mana yang perlu kamu baca?”

Use a short notification collage from invented accounts, then cut to the actual
product. The visual contrast is clutter becoming a source-linked brief—not a
falling portfolio magically recovering. AI-generated cinematic material may
illustrate the problem, but must never impersonate a customer, an actual market
event, or evidence that the application worked.

## One-minute teaser

The required teaser is a recording of the product working. Keep recorded UI
dominant; motion graphics support it rather than replacing it.

- 0–6 seconds: hook over real source-selection UI, with a brief notification
  collage transition if useful.
- 6–18 seconds: choose sources and show a supported configuration being saved
  and confirmed by the real backend.
- 18–34 seconds: show the autonomous trigger, Sectors-backed context and a
  source-linked research result. Only show features actually connected.
- 34–50 seconds: show a real unattended run, timestamp and confirmed delivery
  destination. Label any recording time jump clearly.
- 50–60 seconds: end on the readable brief and Bursawatch mark:
  “Pantau sumbernya. Tetap pegang keputusanmu.”

## Judging video, at most three minutes

- First 20 seconds: intended user and concrete problem.
- Next 40 seconds: real setup, authorized save and read-back.
- Next 50 seconds: schedule/trigger configuration beside real unattended run
  evidence, including configuration revision and applied state.
- Next 40 seconds: where Sectors data changes the result; source timestamps,
  attribution, no-change outcomes and a truthful failure/recovery example.
- Final 30 seconds: actual delivered output, benefit and clear boundaries.

Record the working path before adding effects. Use Remotion only as an editing
option, not a requirement: captions, deliberate crops, transitions and brand
end-card. Do not buy video-generation subscriptions before a short quality test
and explicit budget approval. No video-generation pricing or trial was verified
in this pass. No finished submission video has been produced yet.

## Evidence and eligibility

The official weights are usability 40%, video/storytelling 30%, and technical
execution 30%. Track 02 needs unattended execution evidence, not just a manual
run. Sectors must be a core source. Automated trades are prohibited. Preserve
truthful provenance and ask organizers about prior-project reuse; changing
commit dates cannot establish eligibility. Obtain approval before making the
repository public or submitting; submission freezes further changes.
