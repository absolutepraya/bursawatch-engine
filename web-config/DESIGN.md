Bursawatch is a **minimal market workspace**: quiet, precise and source-led. Use Hanken Grotesk Variable for every heading, label and control; no Inter, Arial, Roboto, system-font branding or Space Grotesk. Keep headings compact, with no eyebrow labels. The palette is charcoal `#141615`, surface `#1c1f1d`, ink `#edf0eb`, muted `#a9b0a9`, copper `#dea777` and border `#383e38`, implemented through semantic CSS variables. Surface contrast supplies depth; no decorative gradients, glow, grain or ornamental chart lines. Detailed tokens and component rules live in [docs/DESIGN_REFERENCE.md](docs/DESIGN_REFERENCE.md) and `src/app/globals.css`.

The authenticated operator workspace has seven flat destinations in its desktop sidebar and mobile bottom navigation: Overview, Sources, Workflows, Jobs, History, Published and Account. On mobile, keep them in one horizontally scrollable row with whole-word labels, and bring the active destination into view. Sources sits directly below Overview and has Securities, Institutions, and People & Org tabs. Institutions use a two-column 4:3 image grid; People & Org uses a three-column profile grid where space permits. Catalog records come from the authenticated API. Curated images are presentation assets; source configuration and effective subscriptions come from the backend. Show each registered endpoint identity-verification state beside its address, separate from subscription state and run health. Current profiles require an API read, and public avatars require consistent platform and canonical URL/handle identity. Show each supported workflow's input, processing and output without adding unsupported settings. Global schedule controls live in Jobs; show desired, reconciled and observed values separately, name affected components before a shared-job save, and keep fixed jobs and viewer sessions read-only. Give recorded activity one dominant visualization and show its coverage limits next to it. Published is a forward-only, cursor-paginated view of confirmed News and Swing delivery records, with server-side date, source, type, route, ticker and group filters plus explicit publisher coverage. Keep all configuration fields grouped by task, with labels, inline errors, a clear save action and unsaved-change protection. Use native controls, visible focus, 44px targets, readable contrast and safe-area clearance. `/app` mirrors these seven destinations without sign-in, using the shared workspace components and labelled in-memory sample records. Preserve public source identities and do not invent credentials, AUM, returns, customer counts or uptime.

Motion budget: **at most two moments**: one short page-entry fade and primary-button press feedback. Everything else uses quiet state-color changes; no staggered card dances, scaling images, looping scans or animated market data. Respect reduced motion. Audit every component against this brief, the UI/UX design skill and Nielsen’s ten usability heuristics: visible status, familiar language, user control, consistency, error prevention, recognition, efficiency, minimalism, recovery and contextual help. Require one obvious action, no nested card clutter, no clipped controls at 200% text size, and no false claim of connection or delivery. Signal Fold refinement 02 is the approved production mark, grounded in “Watch the sources. Keep the judgment.” Preserve its three source inlets, diagonal fold and negative space across navigation, favicon and avatars.

Configuration browsing: start with source category, then search and platform/status filters. Source cards open a focused account/content view. Keep identity editing and collection evidence secondary to choosing content. Explain the separate roles of Sources, Workflows and Jobs beside the catalog. Workflow rows show their input, processing and output before opening. Platform filters reflect registered accounts, including RSS feeds. Filters never alter a draft or hide a focused account while editing. Adding a workflow account clears filters, opens its editor and focuses its first field. Validation brings hidden fields back into view. Show a sticky save/discard bar only while there are unsaved changes or a save is in progress; include a change count and reserve the measured mobile navigation height. Content controls use explicit On, Off and inheritance choices and initialize from their selected scope. Pointer-only primary save feedback uses a 0.96 scale over 160 ms with cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1); remove both transition and transform for reduced motion.
