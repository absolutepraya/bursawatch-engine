The palette is charcoal `#141615`, surface `#1c1f1d`, ink `#edf0eb`, muted `#a9b0a9`, copper `#dea777` and border `#383e38`, implemented through semantic CSS variables. Surface contrast supplies depth; no decorative gradients, glow, grain or ornamental chart lines. Detailed tokens and component rules live in [docs/DESIGN_REFERENCE.md](docs/DESIGN_REFERENCE.md) and `src/app/globals.css`.

The authenticated operator workspace has five flat destinations in its desktop sidebar and mobile bottom navigation: Overview, Sources, Workflows, History and Account. Current profiles require an API read, and public avatars require consistent platform and canonical URL/handle identity. Show each supported workflow's input, processing and output without adding unsupported settings. Schedule controls sit inside that workflow's configuration detail; distinguish saved schedule intent from confirmed effective state. Give recorded activity one dominant visualization and show its coverage limits next to it. Keep all configuration fields grouped by task, with labels, inline errors, a clear save action and unsaved-change protection. Use native controls, visible focus, 44px targets, readable contrast and safe-area clearance. Preserve authentic source identities and do not invent credentials, AUM, returns, customer counts or uptime.
