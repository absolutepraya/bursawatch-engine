# X Post Watch runtime contract

The watcher polls RSSHub's RSS/XML user feed every five minutes for each enabled profile. Post classes are normal, quote, reply, repost, article, and ambiguous. Configuration determines the first four; standalone X Articles are always ignored, and ambiguous posts are skipped with a degraded heartbeat. An authored post which links to an Article remains eligible but its Article card and text are removed. An authored quote of an Article retains a compact quoted-Article label, `Read Article on X` link, and Article cover or preview media, never Article body text. `thread_handling.self_chain` holds a candidate for its configured quiet window after observation, then combines up to 10 same-author quote or reply continuations found in the user timeline over the preceding four hours. `thread_handling.disabled` sends each eligible post immediately without holding or assembling a chain. Replies to other accounts still follow `forward_reply`.

`enable_llm_title` controls whether the agent supplies a source-grounded Indonesian title. When present, every rendering begins with `### <:twitter:...> <title>`, then a muted byline `-# <:profile:...> <display name>`. Titles are one line, five to 120 characters, with no link or ending punctuation. `id_stock` and `us_stock` titles begin with the relevant exchange ticker and a colon, for example `MYOR:` or `META:`. Macro titles are natural and never invent a ticker. When title generation is disabled, the legacy profile heading remains as the safe fallback.

`discord_channels` is the per-profile ordered list of `{key, channel_id, description}` destinations. A non-routing profile must have exactly one channel. A routing profile must have at least two and the agent may return only one configured key, which the scanner validates and deterministically selects. Current Ricky keys are `macro`, `id_stock`, and `us_stock`. Classify the central thesis, not names mentioned in the source. Macro covers economy-wide and cross-asset conditions, leverage, derivatives, liquidity, market-wide valuations, investor positioning, bubbles, and broad sector or AI-cycle risk, even when companies or ETFs are examples. `id_stock` is only a direct IDX-listed issuer or ticker thesis. `us_stock` is only a direct NYSE- or Nasdaq-listed security thesis, including ADRs. When a central ticker's issuer or listing is unknown or ambiguous, the agent looks it up through Yahoo Finance, then Serper, then Brave Search. Lookup metadata may determine only issuer identity, exchange, listing country, exact exchange ticker, and route; it must not add outside facts to the source-grounded title or summary. An inconclusive or conflicting lookup falls back to macro without guessing. A direct company thesis outside those two investible universes also falls back to macro until a dedicated channel exists. If removing company names leaves a broad market thesis, it is macro.

`enable_llm_relevance_filter` requires the agent to make a closed relevance decision before title, summary, or routing. Substantive economy, business, capital-markets news, analysis, opinions, market education, and investing views are relevant. Surveys, promotions, greetings, personal updates, event invitations, generic engagement, and unrelated random posts are not. `additional_prompt_instruction` is a validated trusted profile refinement, appended to the common instruction when non-empty. A deterministic safety guard marks direct ticker disclosures, earnings, corporate actions, dilution, rights issues, private placements, and `#RangkumKeterbukaanInformasi` / `#RangkumReport` posts as always relevant. The agent receives this guard and the scanner rejects an irrelevant decision for it. An accepted irrelevant decision removes the active leased event without Discord delivery and increments the next heartbeat's `filtered` count.

For profiles where `enable_llm_summary` is `false`, the raw normal post appends `[View on X](<url>)` to its final non-empty text line. Quote output starts immediately after that line as a Markdown blockquote containing the quoted author in bold, quoted text capped at 400 characters on a word boundary with `…` when cut, and `[View quoted on X](<quoted-url>)` appended to its final non-empty line. A quoted paragraph break uses `> ` plus a zero-width space so Discord does not render a bare `>` marker. Long source text splits at a blank line, line, word, then hard character boundary below Discord's 2,000-character limit, with the heading only in the first leg. View links and quote blocks are atomic and may become a separate message instead of splitting. A collected thread renders root to latest. An authored quote of an X Article renders its compact label and `Read Article on X` link, then its cover or preview media, without Article body text. The same compact Article block remains visible in summary mode. Media follows text as independent messages: thread media in order, then external quoted-post media.

For profiles where `enable_llm_summary` is `true`, the deterministic scanner claims one durable analysis event and supplies the ordered combined thread text, latest URL, and optional external quote context to the Hermes agent. The agent returns the required validated Bahasa Indonesia title and summary, never posts directly, and never sees or mutates watcher state. The sent format is only:

```md
### <:twitter:...> <title>
-# <:profile:...> <display name>

*(Ringkasan)* <paragraph one>

<optional paragraph two>

[View on X](<main post URL>)
```

The raw post and quoted post are not rendered for summary-enabled profiles. Summary output is one or two short paragraphs, must start with exactly one `*(Ringkasan)* ` label on paragraph one, is direct rather than third-person narration about the writer, and is capped at 1,600 characters. A repeated label at the start of paragraph two is removed during validation; any other duplicate label is rejected. Thread media and external quoted-post media follow in that order.

State is versioned and stores a per-profile cursor plus FIFO delivery outbox and an accumulated filtered count. LLM analysis jobs have a 15-minute agent lease and are retried from durable state after lease expiry. Source failure does not advance a cursor. Text and each media item are independent persisted legs. Every scheduled run posts `🫀 x-post · HH:MM WIB · <tokens>[ ⚠️]` to `#hermes`; fatal failures use `❌ x-post · HH:MM WIB · failed: …`.
